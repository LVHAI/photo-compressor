Frequently Asked Questions

How does PhoSlim compress photos?

PhoSlim uses advanced image encoding to reduce the file size of your photos while preserving visual quality. You can choose from presets (Light, Standard, Max) or customize quality, format, and other settings manually.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. All photo processing happens entirely on your device. Your photos never leave your iPhone or iPad. PhoSlim does not require an internet connection and does not collect any personal data.

Where do compressed photos get saved?

Compressed photos are saved directly to your Photos album. You can find them alongside your other photos in the Photos app.

Why is the compressed photo sometimes larger than the original?

Photos that have already been heavily compressed (e.g. downloaded from messaging apps) may not benefit from further compression. When this happens, PhoSlim automatically keeps the original file to avoid making it larger.

Why does PNG output become larger?

PNG is a lossless format that preserves every pixel's original data. Compressing a JPEG image and saving it as PNG will usually result in a larger file. For best results, use JPG or WEBP for photos, and reserve PNG for images that need transparency.

How many photos can I compress at once?

Free users can compress up to 3 photos per day. Paid users can batch compress up to 20 photos at once with unlimited daily usage.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchase". Your purchase will be restored automatically if you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

Does PhoSlim work offline?

Yes. PhoSlim works entirely offline. No internet connection is required for compression.

Which image formats are supported?

PhoSlim supports all common image formats including HEIC, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP. Output format can be JPG, PNG, or WEBP.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
